SIGNAL · DESIGN ROUND 1 · DIRECTION B

Instrument

Signal as an instrument panel for demand: separate signal from noise with dense, aligned, numeric reading. Graphite surfaces, one sharp orange, numbers set in mono.

HOW IT SERVES THE BRIEF
  • AI proposes, people decide: every AI output is a dashed cyan frame with a PROPOSED tag and a 3-step confidence readout. Accepted turns solid with a check, edited carries a ± diff (AI value struck, PM value shown), rejected collapses grey and struck through. Same tags on the public submit page.
  • Customers' words kept: verbatim quotes sit in a ruled quote block in the body face, with account and plan in mono metadata.
  • Demand at a glance: right-aligned tabular counts, a segmented meter and a HIGH / MED / LOW band word. Each status pill has its own icon shape.
  • Dark is an equal: both themes are tuned separately, not inverted.
RISKS
  • The density suits PMs but can feel cold or technical to a customer on the public board.
  • Too many mono labels turn into noise. They need to stay limited to metadata and counts.
  • Orange can read as "warning". It is kept off error states, which use red only.
  • The AI cyan sits near "In progress" blue. The dashed outline and icon shapes carry the difference, not hue.

Mark: a solid block of noise with one notch cut out, and a single orange dot lifted into that notch. It means "one signal isolated from the field". It is built from a square and a circle, with no arcs, bars or waves.
