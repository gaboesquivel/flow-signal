Signal · Design round 1 · Direction A

Ledger

Signal as a well-kept ledger of what customers need: calm, paper-like, text-first. AI writes in pencil; a person's decision turns it to ink.

Serves the brief
One rule everywhere: anything the AI proposed sits behind a dashed margin rule with an italic small-caps Suggested label and a lighter graphite-indigo ink (--ai-foreground). Accepting draws a solid rule and normal ink; edits carry a small edited note; rejects are struck and faded. Customer words are pulled quotes with a heavy solid rule, so quotes, AI text and confirmed facts never look alike. Supporter counts sit in a right-hand mono column like ledger figures; demand is three tally pips plus words; status is a distinct shape plus a text label.
Evolves
Keeps today's paper #f7f7f2, deep indigo and sage-grey family. It drops boxed cards for hairline-ruled rows and adds a serif (Newsreader) so need statements read as sentences.
Risks
Low-chroma pencil vs ink can be subtle on poor screens, so it leans on the dashed rule and the label, not colour. A serif-heavy UI can feel slow or "bloggy" for a daily PM tool, and dense operator tables may need a tighter mode. With little colour, the primary button carries most of the hierarchy. Dark mode loses some of the paper metaphor.

Identity sheet

Mark: three short ledger rules. The outer two converge into the middle one, which carries on as a single line: many voices filed into one need. Wordmark: "Signal" in Newsreader Semibold.

1a · Public board

A ruled ledger, not a wall of cards. Each statement reads as a sentence; the people count sits in the right margin like a ledger figure. Summaries and decisions are published ink.
