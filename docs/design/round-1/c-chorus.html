Design round 1 · Direction C

Chorus

Many voices becoming one clear need. People and their own words are the hero; Signal is the quiet assistant that listens and suggests.

How it serves the brief

  • AI proposes, people decide: every suggestion sits in one honey-tinted panel labelled “Suggested by Signal”. Accepting turns it into a plain white card with “Confirmed by you”; edits add “Edited”; rejects collapse to a muted struck line.
  • Customers' words are kept: verbatim quotes are coral-edged speech blocks, never tinted as AI.
  • Demand at a glance: a person count with an avatar stack, plus a “chorus” of one dot per supporter and a written band label. Status pills always carry an icon and a word.

Risks

  • Honey AI tint could be read as “warning” by some PMs. Mitigated by the label, the mark and no warning icon, but worth testing.
  • The chorus dots stop scaling past ~30 supporters; they'd need a cap (“30+”) and must never stand in for the number.
  • The warmth can feel soft in a dense daily console. The operator view keeps type smaller and rows tighter to compensate.
  • The public board shows anonymous avatars, never initials, so supporters can't be identified.

Identity sheet

The mark shows three voices overlapping into one shared centre, the need. Shown at 64, 32 and 16 px, with a favicon in a browser tab, on light and dark. Below it: the AI state language, the status pills and the demand bands.

1a · Public board /

Calm and welcoming for someone visiting once. Public fields only: no submitter names or verbatim text, and avatars are anonymous.

1b · Submit with live duplicate check /submit

The person's words stay in their box. Signal's restatement and match reasons sit in the AI panel, in its own quieter voice. On a phone the panel moves directly below the textarea.

2 · Operator triage /operator/triage

Each pending suggestion is a tinted panel. Facts it points to (existing needs, a customer quote, a PM override) are white cards inside it. The resolved item at the bottom shows how a suggestion looks once a person has confirmed it.
