Signal · Design round 2 · Variant 1

Page

One thing at a time. Every screen is a single quiet page in one centered column: one serif question, what you need to answer it, and one primary button.

Idea
Submit is four pages, not a form with a side panel. Step 2 gives each match the full width of the column so it can be read, not scanned. The board is the same column as a ruled list. The PM console uses a focus mode: one suggestion at a time with everything needed to decide it, and the rest of the queue as a slim list in the margin.
Principles
  • Minimal: no cards, no pills, no stepper graphic. Progress is plain text ("Step 2 of 3"). Rules are hairlines; the only heavy rule marks what is selected or current.
  • One primary action per screen, always bottom-right; Back is a text link.
  • Writing welcome: step 1 is only the person's words, in serif, with room to write. Their words are kept verbatim and shown to PMs as a pulled quote.
  • Pencil / ink unchanged from Ledger: AI text sits behind a dashed rule in graphite-indigo; anything a person approved is ink with a solid rule.
  • Same taste for PMs: triage is one decision at a time with J/K/A/E/R; Needs is a plain ruled table, figures right-aligned in mono.
Risks
Focus mode hides the shape of the queue, so a PM can't compare two suggestions side by side (the margin list helps only a little). Four pages feel slow to someone who already knows the board has their need. The narrow column wastes width on large screens, and the Needs table is the one place where density beats calm. With so little chrome, the single indigo button carries all of the hierarchy. The "second ask" line in step 2 is not shown: the example has none.
