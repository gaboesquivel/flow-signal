Signal · Design round 3 · Prototype 4c · Compare direction

Board, criteria, metrics, chat

The PM's overview. What is most worth doing, how "worth" is weighed, whether Signal is working, and one question answered from the data. Everything below is clickable.

Steps
  1. Needs board. 23 needs in a ruled table, sorted by priority. A dashed circle marks needs with suggestions pending in Triage.
  2. Filter and sort. Filter by theme and status, search the statements, and sort by priority, people, accounts or ARR (click a column header).
  3. Open a row. The row opens inline. It shows the two most urgent verbatim signals with person, account, plan, ARR and urgency, the score breakdown at the current weights, and the decision if there is one.
  4. Criteria. Move the four weights. The preview re-ranks the scored needs live and a pencil note explains what moved. Save stays disabled until the weights sum to 1.00, the same rule set_criteria enforces.
  5. Save. The weights turn to ink and the Needs board re-ranks. Undo restores the previous weights.
  6. Metrics. Shows the three MVP metrics as big numbers, with people and agents counted apart. Baselines are unknown. Below them come the guardrails and the supporting counts. You can switch between 28 days (the API default) and 90 days (the length of the seed).
  7. Chat. The Operator asks "What are enterprise accounts blocked on?". The answer comes back as pencil prose, and each need it mentions is a link. The cited needs follow as a ruled list. Clicking one opens it on the Needs board.
Operations
get_demand_summary, search_needs, list_suggestions, get_need_detail (board and open row) · get_criteria, set_criteria (criteria, and Undo as a second set_criteria) · get_metrics with from/to · get_demand_summary with accountId (chat).
Not in API
  • One operator list of needs. No single operation returns every need with its score, accounts, ARR and pending count. The board combines get_demand_summary (live needs only, and it reports ARR plus open deals together), search_needs (shipped and declined needs) and list_suggestions (pending markers). The open row is one get_need_detail call.
  • Bulk criterion scores for the live preview. Re-ranking before Save needs each need's per-criterion scores. Today that means one get_need_detail per scored need, and the arithmetic runs in the client.
  • AI explanation of ranking impact. get_criteria and set_criteria return only weights. The pencil "What changes" note has no operation behind it.
  • False-match rate. get_metrics does not compute it. The page shows rejected merge suggestions (from suggestionAcceptance.byKind) as the nearest count and labels it as a stand-in.
  • Filter demand by plan. get_demand_summary filters only by accountId or themeId. For "enterprise", the chat calls it once for each Enterprise account (Acme, Northwind Traders, Globex) and merges the results.
Risks
Only 4 of 23 needs have scores in the seed, so a board "sorted by priority" is mostly sorted by supporters. The criteria preview can only move those four. In the seed, prevention rate is close to 100% by construction (every supporter after a need's first joins it at submission). On real data the number will be much lower. Chat prose is AI and stays in pencil, and nothing in it is confirmed. The needs table on a phone drops the theme, accounts and ARR columns.
Content
All needs, accounts, plans, ARR, asks, scores, decisions and suggestions come from the demo seed (demo-data.ts and demo-workspace.ts). People's names and urgencies come from the seed's fixed faker run. Metrics were computed from the seed's event log using get_metrics' rules. Invented: the prose in the chat answer, the pencil explanations on Criteria, the short need names used in that prose, and the example weights in step 4.

The flow

The strip jumps to each state, and you can also click through inside the frame. State is real: filters, sorting, the open row, weights, saves and the chat all change what renders.
