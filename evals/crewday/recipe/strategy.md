# Crewday product strategy, adoption year

Hidden memo. Written by Crewday's head of product before Signal was turned on and before any signal arrived. Every need's `truePriority` and `strategyDecision` in both worlds follows from this memo. Month 0 is the month Crewday adopts Signal; month 11 is the last month of the year.

## Where we are

We lost three Enterprise logos last year. Two left after labour-law fines they blamed on our schedules; one left after a quarter of payroll corrections. Enterprise gross churn was 14%. Growth customers who try to move up to Enterprise stall on the same two questions: "will this keep us compliant?" and "will payroll be right the first time?"

## Focus themes for the year

1. **Compliant by default (`compliance`).** A schedule that Crewday lets you publish should be legal: predictive-scheduling notice and premiums, rest periods between shifts, break rules, minors' hours, and credentials that must be valid before someone works a shift. Every rule needs a record an auditor accepts.
2. **Hours to pay with no rework (`payroll-exports`, and the parts of `time-attendance` that feed it).** Approved hours should reach payroll correctly the first time: pay rules, differentials, corrections after approval, and exports to the payroll providers our customers actually use.

Work outside these two themes is planned only when it removes a blocker for a churn-risk Enterprise account or an open Enterprise deal.

## Revenue focus

- **First: keep Enterprise.** Reduce Enterprise gross churn from 14% to under 8%. A need raised by a churn-risk Enterprise account outranks a need with ten times the votes from Starter accounts.
- **Second: land up-market deals** in healthcare staffing and logistics, where open Enterprise deals of $150k or more are in flight.
- **Third: Growth expansion** to Enterprise.
- Starter demand counts as a tie-breaker, not a reason.

## What we will not do this year

- Build a payroll engine. No gross-to-net, tax filing, or pay slips. We export to payroll; we do not run it.
- Build an HR suite: recruiting, applicant tracking, performance reviews, or onboarding paperwork beyond the credentials a shift requires.
- Consumer-style social features: team chat, feeds, kudos, badges, or gamification.
- White-label or custom-branded apps, or a native desktop app.
- Fully automatic scheduling that publishes without a manager. Assistive suggestions only.
- One-off features for a single Starter or Growth account, or bespoke modules for one vertical (menus, room inventory, ride routing).
- Cosmetic work (themes, dark mode, custom colours) unless it fixes accessibility.

## How we rank

A need's rank is set by, in order:

1. Fit to a focus theme.
2. Revenue at risk or in reach: ARR of churn-risk Enterprise accounts that need it, then open Enterprise deals.
3. Breadth of revenue across Growth accounts.
4. Volume of requests, as a tie-breaker only.

Anything on the "will not do" list ranks at the bottom regardless of volume.

## Decision capacity

Engineering can take on **12 needs per quarter (48 for the year)**. The PM plans needs in rank order. A need is planned in the first quarter that starts after it has been visible for a month and still has a free slot; its decision is due by the last month of that quarter. Needs imported from the old spreadsheet have been visible for a long time, so they can take first-quarter slots. A need that does not get a slot this year is declined ("not this year"), and the decline is due within three months of the need first appearing, so requesters are not left waiting.
